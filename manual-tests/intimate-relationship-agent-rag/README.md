# Intimate Relationship Agent + RAG Manual-Test Pack

This folder contains two contrasting classroom Agents for the topic of building intimate relationships.

## Important separation

- Upload `AGENT.md` through the Agent persona editor. It defines identity, values, speaking style, reasoning habits, and debate strategy.
- Upload the files inside `rag/` through the knowledge-library interface. Enable them in that Agent's draft and index them before publishing.
- Do not upload `AGENT.md` as RAG evidence. Do not paste the research summaries into `AGENT.md`.

## Agents

### Adrian Vale

- Persona: an experienced, charming, self-aware heartbreaker.
- Knowledge emphasis: attraction, novelty, shared activities, self-disclosure, and the distinction between chemistry and commitment.
- Upload `adrian/AGENT.md` as the persona.
- Upload and enable every Markdown file inside `adrian/rag/` as RAG.

### Dr. Eleanor Zhou

- Persona: an evidence-oriented relationship psychology specialist.
- Knowledge emphasis: self-disclosure, perceived partner responsiveness, intimacy processes, measurement, and limits of inference.
- Upload `eleanor/AGENT.md` as the persona.
- Upload and enable every Markdown file inside `eleanor/rag/` as RAG.

## Suggested debate topic

> Can lasting intimacy preserve excitement and independence without sacrificing honesty, trust, and emotional security?

## Teaching purpose

The two Agents should differ because of both persona and knowledge selection. The research notes are short, curated summaries for a private manual test. They preserve source links and limitations, but they are not substitutes for reading the original publications and are not clinical advice.
