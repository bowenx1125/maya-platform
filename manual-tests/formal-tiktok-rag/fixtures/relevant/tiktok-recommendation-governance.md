# Recommendation-governance evidence

TikTok's For You feed uses a personalized recommendation algorithm. TikTok says
signals can include a person's interactions and video information, while the Supreme
Court described how personalization and moderation shape what people see. This makes
algorithmic control and transparent safeguards relevant to a classroom policy
question. The evidence is a paraphrase of the formal corpus claims C01 and C03; use
the source manifest for the primary links.
