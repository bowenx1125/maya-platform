# Maya avatar preview notices

## `female_1.glb`

This local demo asset is provided by the audited `lhupyn/motion-engine` demo
asset and is retained only for the private Maya demo. The accompanying MIT
license is in `LICENSE-motion-engine.txt`. The avatar catalog registers this
file together with `elder-scholar.glb` and `noble-scholar.glb`. A template id
selects one of those meshes. Clothing and skin colors come from
`material_theme`, and eyes from `eye_color`. This round does not deliver
skeletal clips or lip-sync. The upstream demo credits Ready Player Me as the
avatar creator; this local inclusion is not a public asset licence clearance.
Recheck the asset terms before sharing or deploying Maya.

## Draco decoder

The files under `draco/` are the GLTF Draco decoder assets distributed with
`three` 0.185.1. They are served locally so preview loading never reaches a
CDN. The decoder build is distributed under Apache License 2.0; the local
license text is in `LICENSE-draco.txt`. Three.js itself is distributed under
the MIT license; its license text is in `LICENSE-threejs.txt` and the
dependency version is pinned in the workspace lockfile.

## Purchased scholar GLBs

`laozi/sumie_bg.jpg` was removed on 2026-11-07. It was an unused backdrop and
was not referenced by the app.

The Debates (beta) stage uses the owner's purchased `elder-scholar.glb`
and `noble-scholar.glb`, converted from the supplied FBX model pack. These
figures have rigged jaws for the initial speech animation and no lip-sync
blendshapes. The owner's authorization covers this private classroom demo;
do not redistribute the model files independently.
